HOW DEAGENSIE WORKS

One connected journey.
From idea to scale.

Build with direction. Connect with capability. Keep moving forward as every stage reveals what your venture needs next.

  1. IDEA

    Bring the ambition.

    Start with an idea, challenge or growth objective. The venture sets the direction.

  2. DEFINE

    Understand the need.

    Clarify the customer, market, objective and requirements before committing to execution.

  3. BUILD

    Shape the foundation.

    Structure strategy, brand, product and technology into a coherent venture blueprint.

  4. LAUNCH

    Enter the market.

    Prepare the offer, channels and go-to-market plan to bring the venture to its audience.

  5. MATCH

    Connect the capability.

    Translate requirements into roles and connect with relevant creative, strategic and technical professionals.

  6. EXECUTE

    Put the plan to work.

    Bring the right people and requirements together to deliver the work.

  7. SCALE

    Learn. Evolve. Grow.

    Build on what works. Strengthen your capabilities and expand into the next stage of growth.

  8. THE CLOSED LOOP

    The loop continues.

    Bring new insights back to the Growth Lab. Refine the plan and connect with the capabilities needed for your next stage.

Every stage of growth reveals the next venture need. The journey continues.
